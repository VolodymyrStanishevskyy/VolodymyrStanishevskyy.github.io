The Early Beginnings

2000s

The concept of FPV flying emerged from the RC airplane community. Enthusiasts began mounting small, low-resolution security cameras on fixed-wing models.

The Rise of Multirotors

2010 - 2014

The "Blackout Mini H" frame revolutionized the industry by proving that small, 5-inch quads were agile enough for high-speed maneuvers.

The Racing Revolution

2015 - 2017

FPV flying went mainstream with the birth of professional racing leagues like the Drone Racing League (DRL).

The Digital Shift

2019 - 2021

For years, pilots were limited to low-quality analog video. This changed when DJI released its Digital FPV System, offering a high-definition 720p feed with incredibly low latency.

Nowadays

Nowadays

Today, FPV technology has evolved into a sophisticated tool used in Hollywood blockbusters, extreme sports broadcasting, and industrial inspections. Modern systems like ELRS provide miles of signal range, while O3-level air units record 4K stabilized video directly on the drone.
